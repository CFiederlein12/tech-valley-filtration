Not Just a Distributor

Tech Valley Filtration was built to fill a gap in the Northeast market: a filtration partner with real technical depth. Not a catalog you order from — a team you can call when you're not sure what your building needs.

Since opening our doors in Albany in 2019, we've grown to serve over 100 commercial, institutional, and industrial clients across the region. Our inventory covers every standard size and MERV rating, plus custom-built options — sourced from proven commercial manufacturers — with same-day will-call available from our Albany warehouse.

What sets us apart isn't the inventory. It's how we use it. We start with your application — the building type, occupancy, HVAC configuration, compliance requirements — and work backwards to the right product. We follow NAFA best practice standards, not just whatever meets minimum code.

Right Product. Right Fit. Every Time.

At the core of what we do is a commitment to getting the filtration solution right — the first time. That means understanding your facility before we quote you anything. It means explaining the difference between MERV 8, MERV 13, and HEPA in plain terms. It means telling you when a more expensive filter is worth it, and when it isn't.

We're based in Albany and proud of it. The Tech Valley region is our home market — we know the hospitals, universities, state agencies, and manufacturing facilities here. And we bring that same approach to every client across the Northeast.

When you work with Tech Valley Filtration, you get a team that picks up the phone, knows your building, and stands behind every recommendation we make.
