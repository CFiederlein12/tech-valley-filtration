Field Service

Bag-In/Bag-Out HEPA Changeouts. Sealed start to finish.

When a HEPA filter has been capturing pathogens, lead dust, or hazardous particulate, the changeout is where exposure happens. Bag-in/bag-out (BIBO) housings keep the spent filter sealed inside a bag from the moment it leaves the housing.

Our team handles the full changeout on site, so your staff never handles a contaminated filter and your space stays protected.

  • Replacement filters suppliedHEPA and pre-filters matched to your BIBO housing, plus the bags and bands for the changeout.
  • Sealed removal and disposalSpent filters are sealed, labeled, and removed for disposal in line with your site’s waste-handling requirements.
  • Changeout documentationA written record of every changeout for your maintenance and compliance files.

How a BIBO changeout works

  1. Isolate the housing

    The system is shut down and the housing is isolated so no air moves across the filter during the change.

  2. Bag out the spent filter

    The old filter is drawn out through the access port directly into the attached bag, which is then sealed and cut free, so the filter is never exposed to the room.

  3. Bag in the new filter

    A new filter is installed through a fresh bag and clamped into place, keeping the housing closed throughout.

  4. Dispose & document

    Sealed filters are labeled and removed, and you get a changeout record for your files.
